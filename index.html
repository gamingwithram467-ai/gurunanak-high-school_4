About Us

Guru Nanak High School, located in Mango, Jamshedpur, is a premier educational institution dedicated to providing holistic education to students from nursery to class 10. Established in [Year, e.g., 1980], the school follows the CBSE curriculum and emphasizes moral values, discipline, and academic excellence inspired by the teachings of Guru Nanak Dev Ji.

Our campus spans 5 acres with modern facilities including smart classrooms, science labs, library, playground, and computer center. We foster an environment where every child can thrive.

Academics

We offer a comprehensive curriculum that includes English, Hindi, Mathematics, Science, Social Studies, and more. Extracurricular activities such as sports, music, dance, and debates are integral to our program to develop well-rounded individuals.

  • Classes: Nursery to Class 10
  • Board: JCERT Affiliated
  • Focus Areas: STEM, Arts, and Values Education

Admissions

Admissions are open for the academic year 2024-2025. We admit students based on merit and availability. For nursery to Class 1, an interaction session is conducted. For higher classes, entrance tests may apply.

Documents Required: Birth Certificate, Previous School Records, Residence Proof, Photos.

Contact the school office for application forms and deadlines.
